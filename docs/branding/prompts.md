# ImageGen prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen` (generation followed by an edit).

## Original generation

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE beautiful professional brand exploration presentation sheet for a Czech mobile-first local culture and events website centred on the village Mostek. Landscape 3:2 composition, large high resolution. A carefully art-directed comparison sheet with THREE equal vertical columns, each its own original minimal identity, separated by very fine warm-gray rules. Warm ivory background #FBF7F1, dark plum typography #211C2B, restrained coral #E45E3D, plus warm teal on the middle alternative. Flat vector-like crisp clean artwork, no photography, no gradients, no 3D, no watermarks, no stock icon collage. Playful, intelligent local humour with refined iOS-friendly simplicity.
Small editorial heading at top exactly "Malý Mostek. Tři velké nápady."
Column 01, strongest recommended direction: exact wordmark "Mostkultura". Custom friendly bold rounded contemporary grotesk wordmark, excellent kerning. Logo mark is a single distinctive thick smooth coral bridge arch suggesting a lowercase m / two bridge arches, with one small offset four-point sparkle above its right side to mean local culture coming to life. Avoid generic music notes/calendar. Clear reduced silhouette usable at 24px. Exact tagline beneath: "Malý Mostek. Velký dění."
Column 02: exact wordmark "Mostkoviny". Logo mark is a minimal warm-teal double arch bridge combined subtly with a speech bubble silhouette, lively rounded geometry, few shapes. Exact tagline: "Co se kde šustne."
Column 03: exact wordmark "Pupek světa". Compact coral orbital ellipse around a single dark-plum central circular point, small satellite, slightly cheeky and confident, original clean symbol with strong optical balance. Wordmark on two lines if needed. Exact tagline: "Všechno se točí kolem nás."
Each column: tiny number 01 / 02 / 03 above, generous breathing room, large standalone logo symbol in top half, confident easily readable wordmark beneath, tagline smaller but readable. Bottom of each column shows TWO small practical application samples: an iOS squircle app icon with white symbol on solid coral/teal, and a compact strictly single-colour dark-plum symbol on ivory showing it works in monochrome. The marks in samples must faithfully match their column's large logo. Add tiny bottom caption "Mostek a okolí" only once centred at foot of whole sheet. Be precise with all Czech diacritics. This should feel like an excellent independent identity designer's first-round logo presentation, not a dashboard or UI screenshot.

## Final edit

Reference: generated first concept sheet. Preserve the three-column layout,
all three logo geometries and Czech names/taglines. Replace the transparent,
dark/glowing background with fully opaque warm ivory #FBF7F1. Remove glow,
shadows, lighting, gradients and transparency. Restore dark-plum #211C2B
text and flat fully opaque coral #E45E3D / teal #268D88 logos. Small application
icons must faithfully repeat their main symbol. Keep all Czech diacritics,
the heading „Malý Mostek. Tři velké nápady.“ and footer „Mostek a okolí“.
